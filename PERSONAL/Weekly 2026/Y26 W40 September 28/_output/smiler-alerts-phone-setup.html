Smiler alerts — phone setup

Two switches on your phone, then a new shoot lands in your hand within seconds of it being posted.

Updated 30 September 2026, 18:25

🔔 ntfy — the instant ping

About a minute. This is the channel that tells you the moment a shoot appears.

  1. Install ntfy from the Play Store (the one by binwiederhier).
  2. Open the app, tap +, and subscribe to the topic name sent in the chat message. Keep it private, it is the address of your alerts.
  3. Stop the phone delaying it: Settings → Apps → ntfy → Battery → Unrestricted. Without this, Android batches the ping and "instant" becomes "when the phone wakes up".
  4. Optional but worth it: ntfy settings → Notifications, turn on pop-ups and give it a loud sound, so a new shoot is hard to miss.
Test it. A test ping went to the topic already. If it did not reach you, the subscription is not set up yet.

📸 Smiler's own push — the backstop

About a minute. This one covers the gap when the Mac is asleep and the watcher cannot poll.

  1. In the Smiler app, open your notification settings (profile area → Notifications).
  2. Tap Allow notifications, and allow it again when Android asks.
  3. Under What to notify me about, switch on New bookings to claim — that is exactly the pool the watcher is watching.
  4. Make them pop up: Settings → Apps → Smiler → Notifications → allow sound and pop on screen.
  5. Same battery fix: Settings → Apps → Smiler → Battery → Unrestricted. On Samsung, also add Smiler to the never-sleeping apps.
Test it. Smiler's own screen has a send-test button. If that test arrives, the backstop works.

⏱️ What happens when a shoot lands

  1. The watcher on your Mac checks the Available pool every 2 seconds. The Smiler app on its own only refreshes every 30, which is the whole reason this exists.
  2. Your phone buzzes within a second or two: the shoot name, city, date and price.
  3. Claim it in the Smiler app → Bookings → Available. First to claim wins, so that is the move to be quick at.
No ping while the Mac sleeps. That is the one gap, and it is what the Smiler backstop above is for.

🧰 If a ping does not arrive

Say so in chat. The watcher keeps a log of every check, so it can be told whether the phone side or the Mac side is at fault, rather than guessing.
