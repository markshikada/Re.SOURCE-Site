Flower touch controls

Gesture upgrade, proposed from a UX research pass.

Bottom line

Hold a word. Keep the chain, add a peek. Hold still and the word’s card previews without committing; hold and drag and the chain builds. The slow hand does the deliberate things.

Swipe across the centre. Yes, it flips to the polar pair. Horizontal, either direction, 40px to commit, only with picks held. Double tap on a word loses flip, because on a phone double tap means zoom.

Harmony. One meaning per start location, in every view and on every device. Two things get fixed: the card’s vertical drag means two different things, and two rows of the written gesture law describe a surface that was never built. Schematics holds its camera still on purpose, so that difference is named, not changed.

Your call, four decisions

Decision 1 of 4

What hold serves

One hold between tap and drag, on the twelve word petals: which half owns the still press.

APeek, then chainRecommended

Hold still previews the card without committing; hold and drag builds the chain. One start, the movement decides.

BIts own menu

Hold opens a small action sheet: hold, flip, undo, open card.

CKeep as is

Hold only arms the chain, and the reference sheet finally says so.

Decision 2 of 4

Swipe across the centre

Polarity flip reaches the artwork by one more route, and a route that collides with zoom retires.

AFlip, and retire double tapRecommended

Across the core, either direction, only with picks held. One route in, one route out: flip keeps four routes and none of them is double tap.

BFlip and keep double tap too

More routes, and the one that collides with zoom stays.

CNo, flip stays where it is

Everything it has today: the F key, the card swipe, the Flip button and double tap.

Decision 3 of 4

Harmony across devices and views

Same hand, same meaning, in every view and on every device; the two breaks sit behind this one.

AOne hand law, and fix the two breaksRecommended

Same start, same meaning everywhere. The card’s vertical drag keeps one meaning per frame and both readings go in the gesture sheet. The two stale rows of the written law get rewritten to what exists.

BDocument the differences only

The gesture sheet names them; behaviour stays as it is.
