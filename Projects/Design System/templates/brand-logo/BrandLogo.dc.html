The mark, and how it is built

Re.SOURCE is built around pattern, colour, word and geometry. Twelve positions sit at thirty degree steps on a wheel. A word system keeps re-deciding which word sits at each one, and a Flower of Life geometry arranges them. It is a personal, long-running research project rather than a product: slow work, no schedule.

The mark is the core and the six Base triangles standing on it. The twelve petals belong to the diagram, not to the signature: they carry a reading, and a logo has no reading to carry. What is left is what survives at 12px — one ring, six triangles, one centre — and it is drawn by a component, LogoMark, so the signature has a single source.

Mark height matches the wordmark's line height, 54. The triangle apex touches the top and bottom of the mark's box, so there is nothing hidden vertically. Sideways there is an 8-unit bearing — 3.6px at this size — so the 24 between the two boxes reads as roughly 28 from triangle tip to R. Clear space is quoted from the box: 24 on every side.

Same geometry, two renderings. The stroke version draws the circle family itself — ring inner and outer as two lines rather than one filled band — and holds to about 24 before it thins out. The solid is the mark. The stroke is for line-art contexts and engraving, where a filled ring would flood. Solid also beats dashed outright below 32: a dash of 1 on 3 goes sub-pixel long before the mark does, and a ring of twelve separate dots merges into a band by 16.

Multiply any figure by size / 120 for a rendered size. The triangles are equilateral, so their base cannot be a whole number: sixty degrees and a whole-number circle do not both give whole coordinates. The system's rule is that a decimal in the result means the logic is wrong — here the logic is right and the decimal is √3, so it is computed at draw time from the marker circle rather than typed into the path.

Rules

LogoMark signs, Mark describes. The signature is the core and its six triangles. The twelve-ring diagram stays on cards and in the flower, where a reading is being shown. Neither substitutes for the other.

The mark is white on black. Never in a position colour, never boxed, never reversed onto a colour. On a coloured ground it is the ground that changes, not the mark.

A triangle points up. Rotation is for animation, not for a fixed alternative orientation.

The markers are equilateral, by construction. Not by eye and not by a base width chosen to look right. Each is inscribed in a 1u circle on r 48, which fixes its apex, its base and all three angles at once.

The wordmark is Figtree 800 with letter spacing left alone. No tracking is declared, at any size.

The full stop is part of the name. Re.SOURCE, never ReSOURCE or RE.SOURCE.
