Leading must be whole pixels on the six unit.

A ratio produces fractional line boxes: 18px at 1.6 is 28.8, which rounds one way on screen and another in print, and never lands twice the same. Each size is paired to one whole number instead.

Letter spacing must not be declared.

Figtree and JetBrains Mono both ship optical spacing tuned per weight. Overriding it replaces a type designer's measurement with a guess, and the guess then has to be maintained at every size and weight in use.

Mono carries data, and italic carries quotation.

JetBrains Mono takes code, coordinates, indices, hexes and every small uppercase label, so a figure reads as measured rather than written. Italic marks a quoted voice, as in the surface is wrong, not the system, and never labels a control, where it would only mean emphasis.

Leading and line spacing are not the same distance.

Leading is the line-height set on one block, so it repeats between every wrapped line inside that block automatically. Line spacing is the gap between two separate blocks (a header and the body copy under it, one paragraph and the next), set once at the join, not repeated. This system draws the join from the same leading numbers rather than inventing a margin, which is why the two are easy to mistake for one thing: leading lives in the type style, spacing lives in the gap between elements.

The halo must be black, with round joins.

Black is the ground everywhere else, so a haloed word reads as sitting on the page rather than as outlined lettering. Round joins keep the stroke off the letterforms at the corners, where a mitre would spike.

A word that grows must recompute its halo at the new size.

The hover progression is plus 5px and 800 to 900, which takes a 36px word's halo from 6 to 7. Leaving the halo at its old value thins it against the larger letterforms, and the ratio is the whole rule.

Colour must sit on a stroke, never behind a word.

Twelve hues at one lightness give twelve different contrast ratios against white text. Holding the ground at black makes text contrast a single fixed value, and the hue then reads as position rather than as emphasis.

A word must be read from Colour LINGO, not from a token name.

Token stems are labels left from an earlier sheet, so a build that takes its vocabulary from one shows the wrong word as soon as the sheet moves. The index is the only stable handle.

05 · Space

A 2:3:6 harmonic, and radius reuses its three numbers

Radius must be 8, 12 or 24, matching space sm, md and lg.

Both scales run on the same harmonic, so a corner set at the step of the padding beside it curves at the rate the layout already moves. A fourth radius would need a fourth spacing step to stay paired with, and the ladder has none.

A pill is the one exception, at half its own height.

Height is 6 units and width is the set's longest word times 3 units, so four characters give 72 by 36, exactly 2:1. Every pill in a set is cut to the same width, because a ragged set reads as data rather than as one vocabulary.

A division must land on a column edge of the twelve.

The ratio picks how many columns the first field takes, and the field measures out to that edge. 3:6 and 1:1 sit exactly on 4 and 6; 2:3 resolves to 5 of 12, and the readout says so rather than burying the rounding in a percentage.

Vertical rhythm must come from leading.

Every gap down this page is a line-height value: 18, 30, 36, 48, 54, 78. Text then sets its own rhythm, and a block moved between sizes keeps landing on the baseline it left.

Measure must come from the span, not from a character cap.

A seven-column field at 18px holds roughly 60 characters at this field width, so a ch cap on top of it only fights the grid at other widths. One constraint decides the line length.

Every layout is authored narrow first.

The base styles are the phone's; width adds by min-width. Written the other way round, a phone inherits a desktop's fixed widths and clips, and the widest case is the one nobody tests on the way in.

Chrome leaves the stage; the instrument keeps it.

On a narrow or short frame the options cluster folds to one chip under the nav and its panels open beneath, so the top of the frame returns to the artwork. A flower, a wheel or a mark is drawn to the clear band between the chrome above and any sheet at the foot, and a zoom is capped by that band. A surface with a nav row of its own ends its band and its columns above that row, so nothing sits on top of anything else.

A controls sheet on a phone is a sheet at the foot, half the frame at most.

Below the field's 720 the options cluster folds to one chip and its panel becomes a sheet at the foot, capped at half the frame: its head and its foot stay pinned and its body is the one part that scrolls, so the close control and the reset stay in reach. The surface behind it reads the sheet's own published height, --rf-drawer-h, and draws its instrument to the band above, so a set is never tuned blind. A short, wide frame keeps the corner card instead, starting below the folded chip and taking the room the chip frees. Mark's instruction, 23 September 2026, quoted before the rule it adds: "Delegate responsive design decisions like modular mobile interface as part of universal brand style." Colour FLOWER's Tweaks drawer is the reference.

A printed card on a phone is a sheet, not a corner.

It rises from the foot at a peek, lifts to the whole card on its handle, and drops on a tap of the artwork. The card is never reflowed: it zooms to the column it is in, min(360px, 100%), so it is one component at a scale on every surface.

Reading text scrolls inside its sheet; the buttons stay in reach.

A chapter or a spec sheet on the foot is at most half the frame, and its body is the only part allowed to scroll, so Continue, Exit and Save never leave the screen. Mark's mobile instruction, 21 September 2026, quoted before the rule it adds: \"make it so it looks good and you can read everything and nothing gets hidden or scrunched up or hidden in a drop down, so all the text is shown and the buttons are visible.\" The sheet reaches a page's reading boxes too: a long prompt on a page like the HQ scrolls inside its own box at most half the frame, so its copy button stays in reach. A legend names the hand the frame has: keys on a keyboard frame, tap and hold where the pointer is coarse.

A row that cannot wrap is a mobile bug, with or without overflow.

Chips, pills, picks and label columns wrap or stack below 720. A three-cell caption row is a stack; a two-column label and value is label above value. A bleed reaches the edge by cancelling the field's own margin, data-bleed, never by a number of its own.

Type stays on the scale on a phone too.

A smaller frame is never a reason for a size below 12 / 18. A specimen drawn at 1:1 that the column cannot hold scales as one drawing, the way the halo above does, rather than clipping or dropping a size.

Look at it at every count, then write the rule.

A probe reports overflow; it does not report a chapter hidden behind a card or a chip stack drawn over a title. Render 320, 390, a phone on its side, 820 and 1440, look at each as a viewer would, and only then commit the rule. A state must lift grey to white, or a fill one step up the ramp.

Press is that same lift, held. A coloured stroke never changes hue on interaction, because the hue is carrying position and would be reporting a move that did not happen. Opacity dimming, scale transforms and colour flashes are all out for the same reason.

A fourth border width means a decision was made twice.

Shadow is wanted in three places: Colour FLOWER's petal ramp, Brand LOGO's mark, and floating UI such as the nav. A black cast does nothing on a black page anywhere else. Both are dialled on one formula and three knobs, read as percentages: Size is reach, Depth is darkness, Spread is softness. The panel and the store are Colour FLOWER's, so what was set there is what shows here, and a change made here is a change there.

Shadow belongs to three things, and depth everywhere else is a grey.

The petal ramp, the mark, and anything floating over the page. A card, a panel or a divider reports its plane by which grey it is made of, so it needs no cast and gets none.

A depth type gets three knobs, and they are the same three.

Size, Depth, Spread, each a percentage, so two techniques that draw nothing alike are still dialled the same way and compared on the same numbers. A fourth knob means the formula was not universal: Drop was cut on 7 September for having no counterpart on the other type.

A depth specimen is shown on g66.

Black on black cannot be judged, so a cast is shown on mid-grey. The values are arrived at by eye and held in one store, which both masters read: there is no second set of numbers to keep in step.

A halo and a focus ring are not shadows.

Both are drawn with the same CSS property and neither reports elevation: a halo carries a word across a busy ground, a ring reports focus. They stay on the greyscale and outside this section.

Duration must be the 60ms unit taken through 2:3:6.

120, 180 and 360 pair with space sm, md and lg and with radius 8, 12 and 24, so a build that knows which spacing step it is on already knows which duration to use. A fourth duration would need a fourth spacing step standing behind it.

Easing must be built from the same two fractions.

Handles sit at 2/6 and 3/6, which makes in place, arriving and leaving one family rather than three curves with separate origins. None of the three overshoots, because motion that reports position is the message and a bounce there reports a move that did not happen.

A hover may overshoot, and nothing else may.

The pop is a fourth curve, cubic-bezier(0.34, 1.56, 0.64, 1) at 360ms, and it is allowed exactly where the motion reports the pointer rather than a position. A mark that answers a pointer with nothing reads dead, and a mark that bounces while reporting a move claims one that did not happen.

Motion must animate transform and opacity, and colour only where a state changes.

Transform and opacity composite off the main thread, so a diagram carrying twelve strokes holds frame rate. Animating width, top or a filter re-lays the page out every frame, and the drop shows first on the slowest device in the room. Colour is the one addition: a state change moves one step up the greyscale, and the ramp is how a surface says it heard the pointer. Background, border and text colour at 120ms, nothing else.

Reduced motion must collapse duration, not remove the change.

A reveal deleted under the preference leaves content that never arrives at its end state. Holding the transition at 1ms gives every reader the same final frame.

An effect is applied, never declared.

Six keyframes, three reactions and a twelve-step stagger live in the compiled stylesheet: arrive, rise, draw, pop, veil, panel. A surface adds the attribute. Before this, nine files each named their own: five easing families where the system declares four, and seven durations off the harmonic.

An entrance waits for the reader.

Animation sits on [data-in], added when a block arrives in view, because an animation declared on the element runs at load and is finished before anything below the fold is looked at. Reduced motion collapses duration to 1ms and never removes the change: a reveal deleted leaves content that never reaches its end state.

11 · Geometry

Three ring slots, and one number that decides the rest

The slots are fixed geometry, and which set fills one is a tweak.

Outer, middle and inner sit on the same twelve positions. Any set in the catalogue can take any slot, so a diagram still shows three at once after a fourth set is added. Fixing the sets instead would make every addition a redraw.

A set must expose one control, and that is Size.

Everything else follows the ratio the Flower of Life implies, so a spread control could only move nodes off their crossings. A control that can produce a wrong drawing is not worth having.

Every node must be a whole radius rotated through a whole angle.

Placement stays reproducible from two integers, which is what lets Colour FLOWER redraw the same figure at any size without a stored coordinate list. Colour FLOWER draws it live.
