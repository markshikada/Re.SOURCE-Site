What each verdict means

Mechanical, in this order · Projects/HQ/FEED-POLICY.md carries the archive rule

  • current — newest of its own name, moved since the cutoff
  • duplicate copy — an older copy of a page that has a newer one
  • superseded — says so in the filename, or a higher version exists
  • stale — untouched since the cutoff
  • archived — already filed under an _Archive folder by hand
