03The four changes now in force

Written into the standing agent rules on 4 October 2026, so they bind every session from the next one onward.

  1. Fan out, do not stream. Three or more independent parts means parallel children in one wave, disjoint write scopes. Say mass-ulw when the ordering between parts is the whole point.
  2. Call workflow skills by name. ulw plan, debug this, review work, visual QA, refactor, remove AI slop. Improvising the same work drops the contract.
  3. Take the receipt before the summary. /diff for the change set, /files for everything touched.
