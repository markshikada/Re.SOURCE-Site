The chat itself. The plain-English front door: answers, judgements, dispatch, and question cards you tap to rule. It holds the judgement model and hands mechanical work to cheaper workers.

Mechanical commander. Audits, sweeps, publishing, repo hygiene, wrap and merge. It stays on the cheap driver and reports blockers instead of guessing.

Craft commander. Owns the visual bar. Where quality matters more than speed, the craft pass takes qwen3.8-flash. Design and reviewer are two seats: the craft pass makes, the reviewer's eye only reports.

The designer's eye. Renders, screenshots and responsive checks. It never edits a file, only reports what it sees.
