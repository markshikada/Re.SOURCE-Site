Privacy policy

This policy covers one thing: a personal application, registered by Mark Shikada, that connects to the Pinterest API for a single account — his own.

In short. One person, one account. Nothing is sold, shared, advertised against, or passed to anyone else. Nothing is built for other users, because there are none.

What the application accesses

  • Reads the account owner’s own Pinterest boards and standard Pins, and only those.
  • Saves curated public Pins to boards the account owner owns — curation of existing content, not the publishing of new content.
  • Never accesses other people’s Pins or boards, never comments, follows, messages, or edits an existing board.

How access is held

  • Access is by a scoped access token stored in the macOS keychain on the owner’s own machine.
  • No password is ever stored by the application or by anyone acting for it. Sign-in is performed by the account owner, by hand.
  • The token is limited to four permissions — boards:read, boards:write, pins:read, pins:write — and can be revoked by the owner at any time.
  • Data returned by the Pinterest API is used to complete a request and is not retained server-side.

What is stored, and where

  • Reference notes — the source address of an image, its credit, and the owner’s own note — are kept in a private local library on the owner’s machine.
  • That library is not published, not shared, and not sold. It exists so the owner can find again what he chose to keep.
  • There is no account system, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party tracking in anything described here.

Sharing

No personal data is sold, rented, or exchanged. Data is disclosed only where the law requires it.

Your rights

Because everything sits on the owner’s own device under the owner’s own account, access, correction and deletion are available by asking directly.
