Desk is the front door: chat on the left, the live page beside it, the estate on the far right. HQ and Wall are retired into redirects, so an old bookmark still lands somewhere true. The seven-link bar that made every page feel like a separate application is gone.

The rule that makes it clear

Every number on the board is read from the machine at the moment you look, and anything baked says how old it is. A page that can go stale must wear its age. That one rule removes most of the confusion the prose was compensating for.

Conciseness is a budget

Chat carries the verdict and the decision; anything longer belongs on a page. Pages are read on a phone, so every claim is one line and every number is generated, never typed, because a typed number is a number that will be wrong.

What is deliberately not built

No dashboard that aggregates the dashboards, and no second place to look. A retired surface becomes a redirect rather than a tombstone, and the inventory is regenerated so the hourly gate notices when one goes missing.
