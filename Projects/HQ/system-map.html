Re.SOURCE · the system, drawn

How it comes together

Six altitudes, read top to bottom. You decide at the top; the work runs downward through the lanes and the seats; the result lands in the House and comes back out to you as something you can open. The dashed line is the only path that matters: the one that ends at your phone.

How to read it

Downward is work. Every arrow is a handoff with a name on it. The band a thing sits in tells you what kind of thing it is: a decision, a seat, a fleet, an authority, a surface, or the ground everything stands on.

Six bands. One dashed line, and it points at you.

What is not on it

The local vault folders are absent on purpose. They are git's working copy — agents need a tree to commit from — and they are not a place you work. The cloud holds the source of truth; the folder is plumbing wearing the costume of a destination.

The three lanes

Intel and Ops are fundamental and universal: they work everywhere. Design is use-case specific. The fourth seat exists because the implementer is never allowed to approve its own work.

Terminal is drawn because it is still the stair, not because it is a destination.
