The 124 tips, and the two you have never seen

Every tip omO can print, read live from omo --list-tips and the definition table inside the installed binary. The ring is all of them; the two red segments are /files and /diff, the only entries your history has never recorded.

Pick a segment of the ring to read that tip.

How the rotation decides, read out of the code

  1. Skip anything already shown this session.
  2. Skip a tip whose keybindings are all unbound on this machine.
  3. Skip a tip whose command is not registered.
  4. The first tip with no entry in your history wins, immediately.
  5. Otherwise the tip with the oldest timestamp wins.
  6. There is no time-based cooldown. Once all 124 are seen it is a pure least-recently-shown loop, forever. A tip you read months ago can come back the moment it is the oldest entry.
