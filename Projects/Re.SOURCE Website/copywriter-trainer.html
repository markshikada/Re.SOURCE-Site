How this works, and the one law

  • Every line gets a verdict. keep means read and better left alone, and the note says why, because a sweep that rewrites everything has no judgement in it. change means the edit above is the replacement. unsure parks it for Mark.
  • A LIVE line is never retitled by hand. It reads off the position table, so a word move in the Sheet renames it by itself. The workbench makes those read-only rather than trusting anyone to remember.
  • Voice: a fellow traveller, never an authority. Sentences rather than fragments. Real words. Nothing that leans on a word that could move.
  • No em dashes, no first-person plural, UK English, and no transformation language. Each line flags its own hits live.
  • The deck is generated, not written here. Re-run the extractor when a surface changes; verdicts survive by line id.
