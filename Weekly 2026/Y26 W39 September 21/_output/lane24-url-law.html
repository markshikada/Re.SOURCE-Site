What it does

A link that opens the master where it says

Send someone a link and the Colour FLOWER opens on the view and the chapter the link names — the walkthrough on chapter three, or the construction view — instead of always opening at rest. A button in the tour panel copies the link to wherever you are standing.

The whole language, in one line

?view=walkthrough&chapter=3

Two things, and only two: which view to open (artwork, schematics or walkthrough) and which chapter (1 to 6, counted the way the surface counts to you — "Chapter 3 of 6"). Nothing else in a link is ever read.

Open that link and this is what you get

The button copies where you are standing

It always writes the page's own address with your place on the end, so the link opens the same spot on a phone or a desktop. Its own label tells you what happened.

What a link may carry, and may never

✓The viewwhich of the three it opens on
✓The chapter1 to 6, only in the walkthrough
✕Your tweakssets, scales, strokes, fills, titles, shadow, glow
✕Your picksa selection is your session's work, not a link's
✕The camerait follows the chapter rather than travelling
✕Sheet and fold statesthe frame's business, read at render

Why: the master's file owns every value it holds. A link carrying values would be a second place the design lives, and the two would drift apart — the exact problem the August audit was about. A link is a doorway, not a copy of the room.
