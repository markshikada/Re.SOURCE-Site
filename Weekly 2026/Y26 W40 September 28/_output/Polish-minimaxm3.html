Twelve cards on the deck lattice

Tap a card to open it. The open card belongs to the lattice at 1440, and rises as a sheet at 390. Another card can be opened without a second click.

How the cards meet

Each card holds a polar opposite. The pair runs opposite ways, so the relationship is the reading, not a diagram.

LIFE↔FIRE

Vitality keeps footing while what is finished is consumed.

TIME↔FLUX

Instability sets the terms; duration keeps the record.

FORM↔LOVE

What gathers decides the shape that holds it.

WIT↔JOY

Insight lands, then the laugh arrives.
